# Investigation of Student Behavior During Free Periods at UCSC

A mixed-methods study investigating how undergraduate students at the
University of Colombo School of Computing (UCSC) utilize their free periods
between scheduled lectures.

## Overview

This study investigates student activities, preferred campus locations,
behavioral patterns, and factors influencing students' choices during free
periods between scheduled lectures.

The study follows a mixed-methods approach, combining qualitative and
quantitative data collection to develop a broader understanding of student
behavior and campus space utilization.

The project was conducted as part of the Research Methods coursework at the
University of Colombo School of Computing.

## Research Question

> How do students utilize their free time between scheduled lectures within
> UCSC premises?

## Research Approach

The study followed a two-phase mixed-methods approach.

### Qualitative Phase

The initial phase involved:

- Structured observations
- Semi-structured interviews
- Non-participant observation
- Identification of behavioral patterns
- Analysis of location-specific and cross-location insights

The qualitative fieldwork examined student behavior across selected UCSC
campus spaces and provided the foundation for the subsequent quantitative
survey design.

### Quantitative Phase

The quantitative phase involved:

- Survey instrument design
- Pilot testing
- Sampling and data collection
- Likert-scale measurement
- Categorical and scaled survey responses
- Descriptive statistical analysis
- Cross-tabulation and comparison of findings

The survey instrument was developed from behavioral themes identified during
the qualitative phase.

## Study Areas

The study focused on selected campus locations:

- UCSC Library
- UCSC Canteen (Bawana)
- Common Rooms (2nd and 3rd)
- Dewale
- Open Area

These locations were selected because they represent different environments
and types of activities available to students during free periods.

## Research Objectives

The study examined:

- How frequently students use different campus locations during free periods
- Activities students engage in during free periods
- Factors influencing students' location choices
- The influence of the next lecture location on space selection
- Student satisfaction with campus facilities
- The relationship between free-period duration and activity choice
